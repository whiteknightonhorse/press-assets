# press-assets

Images used in articles published by the APIbase, Provek, AIPush, DefyCard and Soukbase write-ups.

- Our own images (charts, diagrams, covers, screenshots of our own pages): **CC BY 4.0**. Credit the brand named in the image or caption.
- Stock photos keep the licence of their source (Pexels or Unsplash licence); the photographer credit is in the article caption.

Layout: `/<product>/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>/<file>`. Charts carry their source URL and as-of date inside the image; the SVG sits beside the PNG.
